Step 2: Prepare Your Data

Your CSV file should include:

  • start_date (DD/MM/YYYY)
  • end_date (DD/MM/YYYY)
  • fte (0 to 1, representing actual FTE worked)
  • position (role title)
Important: The FTE value should represent your actual working FTE (e.g., 0.5 for half-time, 1.0 for full-time). The calculator will automatically calculate the disruption (1-FTE) for periods of reduced work.

Grant Application Guidelines

NHMRC Requirements

Relative to Opportunity Assessment

NHMRC assesses your track record relative to opportunity, considering career disruptions that may have impacted your research output and achievements.

Documentation Requirements

You must provide clear documentation of career disruptions, including dates, reasons, and impact on research capacity. This calculator helps generate the required evidence.

FTE Calculations

NHMRC requires accurate FTE calculations to assess your research output relative to the time you were actually able to conduct research.

Best Practices

Complete Career Timeline

Include your entire career history with no gaps. The calculator will help identify any missing periods that need to be documented.

Accurate FTE Values

Use actual FTE values (0.5 for half-time, 1.0 for full-time). The calculator automatically calculates disruption periods (1-FTE).

Supporting Documentation

Keep supporting documents for career disruptions (medical certificates, leave records, etc.) to substantiate your claims in the grant application.

Frequently Asked Questions

NHMRC Investigator Grants

What is an NHMRC Investigator Grant?

NHMRC Investigator Grants support outstanding researchers to conduct innovative research programs. They consider career disruptions when assessing relative to opportunity.

How does this calculator help with grant applications?

This calculator helps you document career disruptions and calculate FTE years, which are required for NHMRC Investigator Grant applications to demonstrate relative to opportunity.

What constitutes a career disruption?

Career disruptions include periods of reduced FTE due to maternity/paternity leave, carer responsibilities, illness, or other significant life events that impact research capacity.

Using the Calculator

How do I interpret the results?

The Relative to Opportunity Results show your actual FTE years per position. The Career Disruption Results show periods where you worked less than full-time (1-FTE).

What if I have gaps in my career timeline?

The calculator will detect gaps and show errors. Ensure your dates are continuous with no missing periods in your career timeline.

Can I use this for other grant applications?

Yes, this calculator can be used for any grant application that requires documentation of career disruptions or FTE calculations.
